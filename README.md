# Helicopter Bullet Biter

Play: https://katmore.github.io/bullet-biter-demo/

Catch helicopter shells in your teeth and spit them back.

## Controls

- Left/Right or A/D: move and face
- Space or Z: bite; spit while holding a shell
- W/S or Up/Down while holding a shell: rotate aim
- Mouse or touch on the playfield while holding: aim freely
- R: restart

Touch controls appear automatically on touch devices and small screens.
